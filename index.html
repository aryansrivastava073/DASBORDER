How the reading is made

The trace below is synthesised from the source frequency, its harmonics, distance attenuation and noise, then sampled at 500 Hz. The spectrum is a real 512-point FFT of that trace. The verdict comes from the spectral peak, matched to the nearest frequency band. This is a baseline method, not the trained ensemble.
